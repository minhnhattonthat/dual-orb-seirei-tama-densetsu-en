# Synopsis - Dual Orb: Seirei Tama Densetsu

Built from the game's own "story so far" screens (`script/system.json`, block "story so far", `0C:DCF6`-`0C:E4AA`)
and the intro crawl, plus short Japanese web summaries. Names as in `glossary.md`. **Spoilers** are marked: don't
hint at them in earlier lines.

## Setting
A world where humans, animals, fairies, monsters and dragons each live as their own kind. Dragons are so powerful
they almost never show themselves and live on only in legend; every creature fears their wrath, and countless races
have been wiped out by it. A dragon is in a sense the gathered mind of all living things, always with two faces
(holy/evil, light/dark, being/nothing). Humans are one race among many, but the only one that decides its own future
by its own will. "A young man met a dragon, and the legend began..."

## Story (surface world, first half)
1. Ralph (`{hero0}`, 15, from a small village) meets the legendary dragon Larzes on Mount Aleitos and is given the
   mission to seal the Panje, the source of evil. He sets out to find three companions: Theo (`{hero1}`), then
   Liz (`{hero2}`), then Lumies (`{hero3}`).
2. The four follow the mystery of ruined Polydos.
3. They learn that Goria, the man who destroyed St. Bacchus, holds a Panje. The "Light of Larzes" (key item) is the
   secret to beating him: they gather Saladin's relics, lay Saladin's soul to rest and win the Light.
4. Larzes then sends the four to the unknown **underground world**: the start of the hard fight.

## Underground world (second half; steampunk: steam machines, railways, a trolley, a remote control)
5. Galba, Liz's father, wants to destroy the world; he has raised a barrier at the Emperor's Mansion with the orb of
   the dragon Digbadinon. To reach him the party fights the **Six Generals** (Rokumasho), each standing for a dark
   side of the human heart.
6. Gieter, who took the Panje when Goria fell, is at Joshua Castle; the party gets the Trolley to go there and
   beats him. Then the Dish brothers (Badish and Radish), then General Gash (they recover the Book of Prayer
   first), then, with the Remote Control from Tizia Castle and free use of the railway, the last generals Eizel and
   Thorn Gina.
7. With every Panje in hand they go to the Panje Shrine to seal them, then to the Emperor's Mansion.
8. **Spoiler (final battle):** Galba is really Larza, the party's father ("their father Galba, no, Larza"). Galba
   took that name when he came to serve underground (0F:9AC7); long ago Larza worried over the two sons he left behind
   on the surface, one of them `{hero1}` (11:80D8); underground he is Liz's father. Keep early mentions neutral and
   translate the source's own hints as they are.

## Who is who (short)
- Larzes: the legendary dragon who gives the mission; formal, ancient.
- Galba: ruler/antagonist of the underground; the Six Generals serve him.
- Panje: the "source of evil", several objects to collect and seal.
- Saladin: a legendary hero of the past (sword, armor, shield, tomb, town).
- Matteson / resistance: a resistance group in the underground; its hideout is attacked by the generals.
